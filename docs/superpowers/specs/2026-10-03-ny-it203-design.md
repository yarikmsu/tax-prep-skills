# Z8 — New York IT-203 and NYC IT-360.1

## Scope and questionnaire (before implementation)

Source-verified TY2025 nonresident and part-year NY returns, with explicit
source allocations and a single continuous resident period. NYC part-year
resident tax includes resident-period AGI and prorated deductions/exemptions.
Mixed-residency joint spouses, special IT-230 allocation and part-year Yonkers
remain refused. Other states' returns and resident credits require their own
work; preparing NY does not clear those obligations.

The user's Z8 request explicitly extends the state-module skill's usual
full-year-only scope. The added shared behavior is a declared filing plan and
a registry variant, not inference from the mailing address. One law cache
prices the selected NY return; it does not pretend to contain other states'
law. Ordinary planners refuse this allocation problem instead of applying
NY resident rates to worldwide wages.

## Inputs, cache and result

`profile.state_filings.NY` names `form: IT203`, covered year, residence type,
resident and NYC periods, joint identical-residency confirmation, completed
obligation inventory and other states requiring returns. It selects NY's law
even when the mailing address is elsewhere. No plan means the existing gates
remain. A plan only clears move/source blocks when all other states are on the
year-valid no-income-tax allowlist and the inventory is explicitly complete.
Foreign moves, statutory second residency and other states' taxable returns
keep their existing blocks/acknowledgment requirements.

Reuse the fetched NY schema: annual deductions, dependent exemption,
household table, rate schedules with published bases, tax table, recapture,
itemized reductions and MCTMT. The whole cache year must match the form year;
no annual amount is inline. Proration by 12 months is form arithmetic.

`new_york_it203` is a registry variant under NY, distinct from IT-201.
`computed_tax_year` proves a computed zero. All-source/source columns, ratio,
lines 32–72 and an embedded `nyc_it3601` worksheet reach PDF/package/YoY.
Two computed NY variants are a hard error, not two refunds or taxes summed.

Income rows carry disjoint resident, nonresident-NY and nonresident-elsewhere
amounts summing to their federal amount; adjustments follow the same rule.
Their raw all-source AGI must equal the computed federal return before each aggregated NY form line is rounded to whole dollars. Signed elsewhere
losses can make NY income exceed the all-source column. Required attestations
cover convenience-of-employer sourcing, special accruals and the complete
modification/credit worksheets. No automatic date-based wage allocation.

## Computation and registration

`compute_ny_it203` derives columns and return arithmetic, prices the existing
NY tax and deduction machinery, caps credits in form order, calculates MCTMT,
and settles actual payments. Estimated-tax penalties reduce line 67; other penalties/interest stay on lines 72/70 and never reduce that refund. `compute_nyc_part_year` applies resident-period
income, chart-equivalent whole-dollar proration and published City rate bases.
City itemized and credit worksheets are supplied and explicitly verified.

Registry variant, typed filing-plan validator, setup/compute/parse instructions,
variant-aware tie-out, EIC and local-tax evidence, filing attachments, annual
IT-2105 calendar and IT-370 extension labels are included. Generic projection
and IT-203 withholding forecasts skip with the concrete missing-allocation
reason. Their zero state result is not an affirmative zero NY liability.

## Tests and limits

Hand tests: annual $100k AGI and $40k NY-source income use the ANNUAL $8k
deduction before tax, then .4000 allocation; $25k/$15k gives 1.6667; negative
denominator or numerator gives zero. NYC six-month $50k AGI less $4k prorated
standard deduction uses the rate schedule on $46k, not annualization.
Verify pre/post allocation credits, MCTMT unaffected by City credits, payment
tie-out, mixed spouses, missing worksheets, duplicate NY sections, cache year,
false coverage claims, documented launcher and PDF/package registration.

No automatic residency determination, convenience test or special accrual
classification; no IT-230 special income percentage, partyear Yonkers,
separate spouse/City residencies, automatic other-state credit or unsupported
other-state return. Those limitations remain visible and gated. This produces
a reviewable summary, not an electronic filing artifact.
