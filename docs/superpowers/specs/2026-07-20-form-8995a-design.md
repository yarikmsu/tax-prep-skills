# Form 8995-A (QBI wage/UBIA + SSTB limitation) — design spec (N2)

Status: implemented. Backlog: 2026-07-20 review, Epic N2.

## Problem

`compute_qbi_deduction` computes the simplified Form 8995 (20% of QBI, the
overall §199A(a) taxable-income cap) and, above the §199A threshold, emits an
`over_threshold` WARNING that the wage/UBIA limitation and SSTB phase-out are
unmodeled. For the persona's high-earning K-1 owners that warning is the last
QBI gap — an over-threshold filer's actual deduction can be far below 20% of
QBI (or $0 for an SSTB), so the unmodeled path OVERSTATES the deduction
(understates tax).

## Scope (v1)

- Model ONE qualified trade or business (attested one-trade-per-entity, or a
  single Reg. §1.199A-4 aggregation the caller has already combined). Inputs are
  keyed at the **trade-or-business level**, NOT the entity level: §199A limits
  apply per trade, a single K-1 can report multiple trades, and combining them
  requires an aggregation election with its own ownership/majority/factor tests.
  A per-entity-only schema would let one wage/UBIA limit span unrelated trades
  and overstate the deduction. Multiple un-aggregated trades are out of v1 —
  surfaced as a caveat; the caller runs one trade at a time or aggregates first.
- REIT/PTP dividends keep their existing 20%-no-limit treatment (Form 8995-A
  Part IV, not subject to the wage/UBIA limit).
- The §199A(a) overall cap (20% × (taxable income − net capital gain)) still
  applies to the sum, exactly as in the simplified path.

## Mechanics (verified vs IRS Form 8995-A instructions + §199A)

Let `TI` = taxable income before the QBI deduction, `threshold` = the fetched
`federal.qbi_threshold` for the status (single/mfj), `range` = the phase-in
range, `excess = max(0, TI − threshold)`, `ratio = min(1, excess / range)`.

**Phase-in range is YEAR-DEPENDENT** (OBBBA §70105 expanded it for TY2026+):
- ≤ TY2025: $50,000 single / $100,000 MFJ.
- TY2026+: $75,000 single / $150,000 MFJ.
Statutory schedule with an optional cache override (`federal.qbi_phase_in_range`)
— the `_resolve_amt_phaseout_rate` precedent, so a fetched figure can correct
drift without a code change. Only the MFJ vs non-MFJ split matters (QSS→mfj is
the caller's mapping, consistent with the threshold keying).

Wage/UBIA limit for a trade: `W = max(0.5 × w2_wages, 0.25 × w2_wages + 0.025 × ubia)`.

Three regimes (per trade):
1. **Below threshold** (`excess == 0`): component = `rate × qbi` (no limit; an
   SSTB is fully qualified). This is the simplified-8995 case — `compute_qbi_deduction`
   already handles it and does not call the 8995-A path.
2. **Above threshold + full range** (`excess ≥ range`, `ratio == 1`):
   - SSTB → component = **0** (not a qualified trade at all).
   - Non-SSTB → component = `min(rate × qbi, W)`.
3. **Within the range** (`0 < excess < range`):
   - Non-SSTB: `tentative = rate × qbi`; `component = tentative − max(0, tentative − W) × ratio`
     (the wage limit phases in proportionally).
   - SSTB (the double-haircut): `applicable_pct = 1 − ratio`; reduce
     `qbi, w2_wages, ubia` by `applicable_pct` FIRST (Form 8995-A Schedule A
     lines 6–7), THEN apply the non-SSTB within-range formula to the reduced
     figures.

Total before the overall cap = `qbi_component + rate × reit_dividends`.
Deduction = `min(total, rate × (TI − net_capital_gain))` (the §199A(a) cap).

## Wiring

- `compute_form_8995a(...)` — standalone, fully validated (strict bool/NaN/neg
  rejection; `is_sstb` explicit bool; threshold/range required when called).
- `compute_qbi_deduction` gains optional `w2_wages`, `ubia`, `is_sstb`,
  `phase_in_range` params. When `over_threshold` AND the wage/UBIA inputs are
  supplied, it DELEGATES to `compute_form_8995a` (replacing the warning with the
  computed limit). When they are absent it keeps the `over_threshold` warning
  (backward compatible — no existing caller passes them). The §70105 minimum-QBI
  floor and the ½-SE-tax netting continue to apply to the QBI base as today.
- `/tax-compute` Step (QBI) collects per-trade W-2 wages / UBIA / SSTB status
  from the K-1 (or Schedule C) when the filer is over threshold and passes them.
- Fetched `federal.qbi_phase_in_range` (optional override) added to the schema.

## Bias direction / degrade

- Over threshold with NO wage/UBIA inputs → keep the loud `over_threshold`
  warning (unchanged); do NOT assume unlimited (that overstates). The filer must
  supply the trade's W-2 wages + UBIA to get the computed limit.
- Multiple un-aggregated trades → caveat (v1 models one); the caller must run
  per-trade or elect aggregation. Not silently combined.
- SSTB status is an explicit attested bool; an unknown SSTB status is the
  caller's to resolve (a wrong `is_sstb=False` on an actual SSTB overstates).

## Out of scope (v1, caveated)

- Automatic Reg. §1.199A-4 aggregation (the election + its tests); the caller
  aggregates and passes combined figures.
- The patron reduction (§199A(b)(7) / cooperatives), REIT/PTP loss carryforward
  netting beyond the existing treatment, and the §199A(g) DPAD.
