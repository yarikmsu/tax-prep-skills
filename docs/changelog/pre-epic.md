## /tax-ptet (backlog #9) — full round-by-round review history

`/tax-ptet` (`agents/lib/ptet.py`, backlog #9) is the FIRST **read-only-AND-writes-nothing** planning skill — it's advisory output (a `/tax-review`-style section), so unlike scenario/roth-ladder/harvest it has no `save_scenario` and no `scenarios/` artifact. It reuses `scenario.extract_baseline_inputs` + `_rate_kwargs` + `read_tax_year` and `rollover._validate_subpath`/`_validate_year_arg`, and ANCHORS the federal benefit the same way the other three do: the entity-level PTET deduction is a negative `other_income` delta through the `compute_scenario` keystone, so the reported $ is the actual marginal effect around `result.json` line 24 (and tapers correctly as a large deduction falls through brackets — you can't save a top-rate on income you don't have). Three PTET-specific primitives: (1) `ptet_tax(state, income, rate_override=)` — CA flat 9.3% vs NY/NJ graduated `(lower, rate, base_tax)` schedules, statutory structural constants cited inline (like tax_calendar's deadline dates), overridable per state; (2) `_election_deadline(state, year)` — reuses the `/tax-calendar` statutory facts (NY's election due March 15 *of the tax year*, can't be made on the return — the trap); (3) the **forgone-SALT offset** = `marginal_fed × min(salt_room, total_ptet)`, defaulting `salt_room=0` (cap exhausted). **Bias direction:** defaulting to cap-exhausted *overstates* savings → loud `ASSUMPTION` note + `--salt-room` escape hatch; valuing the offset at the top marginal rate can only *reduce* net savings (safe under-statement). Per-state benefit is the anchored aggregate allocated pro-rata to each state's PTET paid (sum reconciles exactly). All numeric inputs go through `_strict_non_negative` (rejects bool/NaN/Infinity/<0). State savings is hardcoded $0 with a caveat (the owner's PTET credit offsets the entity tax). Federal-only v1; non-CA/NY/NJ states surface a `modeled=False` "not modeled" note rather than a silent $0. **Three Codex P2 fixes (PR #22):** (1) **§199A QBI clawback** — the keystone computes QBI only from `self_employment_net`, so K-1 income flowing through `other_income` gets no QBI recompute; for QBI-eligible income the PTET deduction shrinks the QBI base (owner loses ~`qbi_rate × PTET` of deduction), which would OVERSTATE savings. Priced as a haircut to the modeled income reduction (`PTET × (1 − qbi_rate)`, stays anchored since the lost-QBI add-back is taxed at the same marginal rate) gated on `--qbi-eligible STATE`; default-off emits an overstatement caveat (don't silently overstate); simplified — ignores §199A income/W-2-wage limits. Gross benefit now allocated pro-rata by each state's *income reduction*, the SALT offset by *PTET paid* (both reconcile). (2) **CA deadline year-aware** — AB 150 (≤2025) June-15 prepayment is a hard election PREREQUISITE; SB 132 (2026–2030) the election survives a missed payment but the credit drops 12.5% (don't tell a 2026+ filer the election is lost after June 15). (3) **k1_states fallback gated on `has_k1_income`** — a stale `k1_states` on a profile with `has_k1_income` false no longer resurrects PTET advice (mirrors `/tax-calendar`'s gating). **Round 2:** repeated same-state `--income` accumulates (`_parse_state_amounts(combine="sum")` — two K-1 entities in one state add up; `--ptet-rate` uses `combine="reject"` since summing rates is nonsensical). **Round 3:** (a) `_PTET_WINDOWS` gating mirrored from tax_calendar (CA 2021-2030 sunset / NY 2021+ / NJ 2020+) — an out-of-window year is `modeled=False` with a note, never a confident price for an expired/not-yet-enacted regime; (b) `--marginal-fed` validated via `_validate_rate` (finite fraction in [0,1]; argparse `type=float` otherwise accepts `-1`/`nan`/`inf`/`1.5` and corrupts the offset). **Round 4:** `--ptet-rate` state codes validated at the parse boundary (`_normalize_state` in `_parse_state_amounts`) + an orphan-override guard in `analyze` (a rate override for a state with no matching `--income` hard-errors) — the escape hatch silently no-op'd on a typo (`California=...` → never matched `CA` → priced at statutory 9.3%). **Round 5 (P1):** NJ BAIT top bracket corrected — P.L. 2021 c.419 (TY2022+) collapsed the legacy 9.12% $1M–$5M + 10.9% >$5M brackets into a single 10.9% over $1M, so the old table UNDERSTATED NJ PTET (a $3M entity by $35,600) and thus federal savings (verified vs nj.gov).
